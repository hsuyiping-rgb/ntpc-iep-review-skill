# New Taipei IEP Review Rules

Use these rules when deciding whether an item is a correction, a reminder, or acceptable. This file has two parts: a **per-section content checklist** (what "complete" looks like) and **decision/process rules** (when to flag, how to verify, how to phrase it).

---

## Part 1: Per-Section Content Checklist

Apply this checklist to every IEP, section by section. It is the substantive standard; Part 2 governs how to turn an observation into (or out of) a finding.

### 學生基本資料 and 參與訂定人員
- 學年度、年級、班級、導師、法定代理人 are filled and internally consistent with every other section's narrative (grade level referenced in 需求評估摘要, service dates, etc.).
- 鑑輔會鑑定文號、特教類別、特教類別加註 are present and plausible against the ability-status narrative (see 診斷/類別比對 in Part 2 — a mismatch is a confirmation request, not an automatic error).
- 身心障礙證明 (有/無) is internally consistent with the narrative — see the 身心障礙證明 rule in Part 2 before flagging.
- 參與訂定人員 includes 法定代理人, 個管教師, and the relevant teachers/specialists; missing categories (e.g. no legal guardian listed) is a correction.

### 一、能力現況、家庭狀況及需求評估
- Seven dimensions should each have a current-status description **and** an implied or stated need: 健康與感官, 知覺與動作, 認知與智力, 語言與溝通, 生活自理, 情緒與社會人際, 基本學習技能與各領域學習表現.
- Test/observation data should be specific (test name, date, score, percentile) and from the current school year where possible.
- 家庭狀況 should describe household composition, primary caregiver, and anything relevant to support planning.
- 需求評估摘要 (優勢/弱勢 + 課程調整/行政支持/個人能力增能) must be concrete enough to trace into Section 2 and Section 3 — this is the anchor for the Logic Alignment Check in Part 2.
- Any safety-relevant behavior (self-injury, aggression, elopement, medical risk) documented here must be traceable into Section 2 (支持策略/特殊生理狀況/特殊情緒行為處理) and Section 4 (BFP) — see Logic Alignment Check.

### 二、特殊教育、相關服務及支持策略
- 特殊教育課程 (1-1/1-2/1-3/無): course hours and 抽離 vs 外加 model should match what Section 1's 需求評估摘要 describes. **Do not read this table without following the course-hours verification procedure in Part 2 — a naive read is unreliable.**
- 相關專業服務 (物理/職能/語言/聽能管理/其他): checked items should list a provider name and frequency, and should match professional-service needs described in Section 1.
- 支持服務 checkboxes (教育輔具, 適性教材, 人力協助, 家庭支持, 適應體育, 無障礙環境, etc.): before flagging an unchecked box as a gap, read that field's own printed qualifying text (see Part 2) — a related-sounding narrative mention is not sufficient on its own.
- 學習評量調整 (評量方式占比調整, 考試服務, 作業調整): adjustments claimed or already in use per Section 1's narrative (e.g. an exam already given with 報讀/延長時間/電腦作答) should appear here, for the same subjects. A narrative describing an accommodation already used in an actual exam is strong evidence for a missing registration.
- 健康與安全欄位 (特殊生理狀況處理, 特殊情緒/行為狀況處理, 特殊醫療需求): any health condition, medication, or emotional/behavioral risk documented in Section 1 should have a corresponding "需要" here, or a clear explanation why not.

### 三、學年與學期教育目標
- Every priority need named in Section 1's 需求評估摘要 should map to at least one annual/semester goal (see Logic Alignment Check).
- Goals should be observable and measurable — a concrete example, checklist, or quantified criterion, not just an adjective like "適當" or "明顯" with nothing to anchor it.
- 評量者, 評量方式, 評量日期 should be filled, and dates should fall within the current school year (a next-year or two-years-out date on one goal, when its siblings all land in the current year, is a classic paste/typo signal).
- **Copy-paste check**: if a student's goal text, evaluator, or evaluation dates are identical to another student's, that is a same-document-class error — verify by opening the other student's Section 3 directly and comparing. Confirm whether the goal content is even plausible for this student's disability category (see the 自閉症 vs 學習障礙 differentiation example in Part 2) before accepting it as merely "duplicated but fine."

### 四、行為功能介入方案 (BFP)
- If Section 1 or Section 2 documents a safety-relevant behavior (aggression, self-injury, elopement, a behavior requiring physical intervention/restraint, a behavior with a documented injury to self or peers), a "學生無此需求" here needs confirmation, not automatic acceptance.
- Where a BFP exists, check whether its 行為問題 description(s) cover **all** the risk behaviors named in Section 1 — a BFP addressing a mild behavior while the ability-status describes a more severe one (e.g. self-injury) elsewhere is an incomplete match, not full coverage.
- Mild task avoidance, anxiety, or attention issues already handled through learning supports do not require a BFP — do not flag "無此需求" by default in that situation (see Part 2).

### 五、轉銜輔導及服務內容
- Required for 小六, 國九, 高三 students only; blank is correct for other grades.
- For required students: 下一階段安置規劃, and the five service-need areas (生涯輔導, 升學輔導, 就業輔導, 生活輔導, 心理輔導, 福利服務) should each have a distinct, specific description — not boilerplate, and not identical across areas within the same student (心理輔導 text equal to 生活輔導 text is a signal, unless independently reverified — see Part 2's disputed-finding guidance).
- （三）執行內容 table (transition goals/services/participants) should be filled when the narrative above it is substantive; a fully blank table alongside detailed narrative sections is a gap worth flagging (soft — not blocking approval on its own).
- **Copy-paste check applies here too** — identical transition text across two different students is a same-document-class error; verify directly against the other student's Section 5.

### 相關測驗
- Do not flag this tab as incomplete purely for showing category headers with no entries — check whether Section 1 already documents the specific test names, dates, scores, and interpretation. If it does, the data exists and is reviewable; only flag when **neither** location has real test data.

## Part 2: Decision, Verification, and Tone Rules

### Correction Items
Flag as a correction only after the Verification Requirement below has been satisfied. When it is:
- A student grade, school year, service period, course stage, or section narrative is internally inconsistent.
- A service or adjustment described in one section is missing or contradicted in another.
- A field explanation appears copied into the wrong adjustment category.
- An annual/semester goal's success criteria are not observable or measurable enough to track — but not a correction if the goal already anchors the criterion with a concrete example or checklist.
- A required section is blank when it applies to the student's grade or service needs.
- For graduating grades, transfer planning is incomplete or lacks concrete next-stage supports.
- Related tests are referenced in narratives with specific findings, but neither the ability-status section nor the related-test tab has any test data.

### Do NOT Flag From Wording Alone
- A goal saying the student will "independently" complete something (e.g. "獨立完成學科測驗") when the same sentence already names the accommodation strategy being used. "Independently" here means initiating/applying the accommodation without being prompted each time — not a contradiction with the accommodation existing. Only flag when the goal text implies the accommodation is being *removed or bypassed*.
- A support-service checkbox left unchecked when the checkbox carries its own printed qualifying condition (a tooltip, an asterisked note, parenthetical scope text) that the student's documented situation doesn't actually meet. Example: 電腦輔具 marked "*學生無法以手寫或使用一般鍵盤滑鼠輸入輸出者，請勾選" — a student who handwrites with difficulty and uses a tablet as a compensatory strategy does not meet that scope. Read the field's own qualifying text, not just its label, before flagging.

### Usually Acceptable — Do Not Flag By Default
- `授課教師：待定` before the schedule or staffing can reasonably be finalized.
- System-built placeholder text, if the user confirms the system inserts it and the teacher cannot remove it.
- Transfer planning for non-graduating grades.
- No BFP when the record describes mild task avoidance, anxiety, or attention issues already handled through learning supports.
- The related-test tab showing only category headers when the ability-status section already documents the test data (see Part 1).
- 身心障礙證明 showing "無" even though 鑑輔會鑑定紀錄 confirms a disability category. These are two independent government systems (教育局 special-ed eligibility vs. 衛生局/社會局 social-welfare certification); neither automatically triggers the other, so this combination is normal. Only flag when the ability-status narrative *itself* states the student already holds a disability certificate while the checkbox says 無 — a same-document contradiction, not a cross-system expectation.

### Verification Requirement (do this before writing any "inconsistency" finding)
A claim that two parts of the record disagree must be traceable to the actual saved capture of **both** sides — not a remembered summary from an earlier pass, not an assumption about what a field "should" say.

- If a required per-tab text capture was skipped for this student, capture it now before asserting anything that depends on it.
- When the user disputes a finding, re-open the original saved capture for that exact field first. Three distinct outcomes, three different fixes:
  1. No real backing (misread, guessed, never actually captured) — retract outright.
  2. Accurate when captured, record since corrected by the case manager — confirm the diff, mark resolved, don't apologize for having raised it.
  3. Still holds against the freshest capture — restate it with evidence cited.
- A diagnostic/clinical mismatch (特教類別 vs. an ICD/ICF code) is not automatically an error — some categories are commonly comorbid. Research if uncertain; phrase as a confirmation request, never a flat assertion.
- A garbled or "file not found" result from a shell tool on Chinese-named files/paths (Windows) can be a tooling/encoding artifact — re-check with a different tool (e.g. PowerShell) before concluding data is corrupted.
- **A record that was rejected and has since been edited (or blanked for rework) by the case manager cannot be used to verify a finding made before the edit** — note it as "無法回溯查證，記錄已變動" rather than confirming or denying.

### Course-Hours Verification (high false-positive risk — follow exactly)
Course-hours table reads of "0" are especially unreliable. In one review batch, 16 out of 16 "特殊教育課程節數為 0" findings were false — the `1-1.普通班學生接受特殊教育課程` table populates via AJAX after the tab renders, and an early read captures a transient empty/`0` state.

A "0 節" claim is only citable once all four of these are on record:
1. The `1.特殊教育課程` radio group itself — which of `1-1` / `1-2` / `1-3` / `無` is actually selected. A page that *renders* like `無` while `1-1` is the selected radio is the exact failure mode behind the false positives.
2. The `1-1` table body expanded, with per-row columns read: 領域/科目, 特教課程提供節數, the three 特教課程使用時間 columns, 授課教師, 起訖日期.
3. A second read of that table, after confirming other dynamic content on the same tab has loaded (e.g. exam-service or professional-service tables show real rows, not just headers).
4. A cross-check against what the narrative says the service should be (see 抽離/外加 mapping below). A match is as reportable as a mismatch — record it in 可保留內容 rather than staying silent.

**Reading 抽離 vs 外加 from the 特教課程使用時間 columns** (they are not interchangeable, and they tell you the service model the narrative is describing):
- `原班原領域` > 0 → **抽離式**: student leaves the general-ed class during that subject's own period. A narrative mentioning separate resource-room grades for 國語/數學 should line up with 原班原領域 hours in those rows.
- `領域學習總節數以外時段` > 0 → **外加式**: taught outside the regular subject timetable (社會技巧, 學習策略, etc.).
- `原班其他領域` > 0 → pulled from a *different* subject's period; worth a confirmation request if the narrative doesn't explain the trade-off.

A narrative saying "外加" against rows carrying only 原班原領域 hours (or the reverse) is a real, specific finding. A narrative saying generically "資源班課程" against any non-zero configuration is not a finding.

### Logic Alignment Check
Do not only check whether required fields are filled — check whether the IEP forms a coherent chain:

**Need (Section 1) → Support/service (Section 2) → Annual goal (Section 3) → Semester goal/evaluation.**

If Section 1 describes a need in any of the seven ability-status dimensions, Section 2 and Section 3 should show corresponding planning. Treat it as a correction when an identified need has no matching support/service, goal, or evaluation method anywhere downstream.

### Differentiating Goals by Disability Category
When goals look templated or reused, check whether their *content* is even plausible for this student's disability category — not just whether the text differs from another student's. Two students with different categories should have qualitatively different priority goals, e.g.:

| | Reading-comprehension SLD | Autism (ASD) |
|---|---|---|
| Core deficit | A specific cognitive process (vocabulary retrieval, text inference); overall intelligence typically normal | Social communication and interaction; not primarily an academic-skills deficit |
| Goals should target | The academic/literacy skill itself: decoding strategy, reading-comprehension strategy, applying comprehension to math word problems | Social-pragmatic communication, emotional regulation, cognitive flexibility around routine changes, generalizing skills across settings |
| Red flag | — | Goals entirely about vocabulary/reading strategy, when the student's academic grades are already solid and ability-status describes social/rigidity/regulation needs instead |

A goal set that is well-suited to one category can be entirely wrong for another even if internally well-written — check it against *this* student's actual documented profile, not just against internal consistency.

### Opinion Tone
These opinions are read by the teacher who wrote the IEP. Every finding — not just the numbered correction list — should read as a respectful request for confirmation, not a declaration that the teacher made an error. This applies even to findings the reviewer is fairly confident about, because the reviewer works from partial/second-hand data and the teacher has full context.

- Prefer: `請補明評量方式及觀察期間，使 115 學年度第 1 學期目標可具體評量。`
- Avoid: `內容不夠完整。`
- Prefer: `提請確認情緒行為障礙與 ICD F84.8 診斷分類是否一致或為共病。`
- Avoid: `特教類別與 ICD 診斷不一致，請修正。`
- Prefer: `能力現況記載已領有身心障礙證明，惟身心障礙證明欄位顯示「無」，提請個管教師確認並更新。`
- Avoid: `身心障礙證明欄位錯誤。`

Separate:
- **主要補正意見**: must be fixed before approval.
- **可保留內容**: content that is already adequate — include this section every time, not only when issues exist; acknowledging what a case manager did well is as much a part of the review as flagging gaps.
- **提醒事項**: useful but not blocking.
